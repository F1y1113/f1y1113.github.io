Yifei Dong

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Systems at the University of Washington, advised by Prof. Zhi-Qi Cheng. I also work closely with Dr. Qi Dai and Prof. Alexander G. Hauptmann. I received my M.S. from Columbia University and my B.S. from the University of Glasgow.

My research interests include embodied AI, world models, vision-and-language navigation, and multimodal large language models.
