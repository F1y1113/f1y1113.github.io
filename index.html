Yifei Dong

Ph.D. Student · University of Washington

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Systems at the University of Washington, advised by Prof. Zhi-Qi Cheng. I also work closely with Dr. Qi Dai and Prof. Alexander G. Hauptmann.

My research focuses on embodied AI and world models, particularly how agents can use language, memory, and imagination to navigate the physical world. I am also interested in vision-and-language navigation and multimodal large language models.

Previously, I was a research assistant at Carnegie Mellon University. I received my M.S. from Columbia University and my B.S. from the University of Glasgow.
